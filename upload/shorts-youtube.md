# Shorts — YouTube upload kit

Vertical cuts of the full films (1080×1920, about 50 s, under the 60 s Shorts limit). Each one's job is to send viewers to its full film.

Render again with `npx remotion render Day0Short out/shorts/day-0-short.mp4 --codec=h264 --crf=18` (same for `ScreenShort`). Framing, clips and hook live in `src/episodes/<episode>/short.ts`.

## Posting plan

- Post one Short a day, not both on the same day. Post Day 0 first, then He Chose the Screen the next day.
- Don't delete or re-upload a Short that starts slowly. Shorts often pick up 2–5 days later.
- Judge a Short by its viewed-vs-swiped-away rate in Studio (aim for over 60–70% viewed), not by its views in the first hour.
