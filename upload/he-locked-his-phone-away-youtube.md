## Why the opening is built this way

This film uses the same hook formula as episodes 8–10:

- **0:00, frame 1:** his hand holds the lit phone over an open drawer while a notification pings. `DAY 1 / 90` is already on screen.
- **0:00.7–0:01.9:** he lets go and the drawer slides shut. The key turns with a loud click that shakes the camera. The caption reads *"He locked his phone away. For 90 days."*
- **The open questions:** what happens to him over the 90 days, and what's waiting on Day 90. The counter keeps the stakes visible, from `REACHED FOR IT: 23` to `DAY 45` (in red) to `NOTIFICATIONS: 2,341`.
- **Why it isn't a remake of Day 0:** that episode was about relapse. This one is about what comes back once the feed is gone. The payoff is that none of the 2,341 notifications mattered, and the people who did matter had already found him.
- **The ending matches the opening:** the same drawer, now open and empty, with the key still in the lock. The last line is "Put it in a drawer tonight."
