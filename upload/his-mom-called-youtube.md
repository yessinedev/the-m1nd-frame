## Why the opening is built this way

Earlier episodes opened on 7 seconds of black stars and text, then a slow, calm scene, and viewers left before 0:11. This one runs on the new hook formula:

- **0:00, frame 1:** "Mom ❤" is already ringing on screen, the phone is buzzing and the ringtone is playing. There's no fade-in from black.
- **0:01.7:** his thumb hits Decline. The ring cuts off, and the counter flips to `DECLINED: 47` in red.
- **0:04–0:11:** a fast scroll through 47 "Declined" rows, ending on *"Then one day, she stopped calling."* That's the open question. It isn't answered until the porch at 2:28, so viewers have to stay to find out what happened.
- **The flashback counts upward toward 47**, month by month, so viewers keep watching the number approach the call they already saw.
- **At 2:54 the hook repeats in reverse:** the same phone in the same spot, and this time he presses Accept.
