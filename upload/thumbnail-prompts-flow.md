# Thumbnail prompts for Google Flow

One prompt per film, in the style of the reference: a bald, round-headed cartoon character up close, a huge exaggerated emotion, a dark scene lit by one strong light, and 2–4 words of thick white brush lettering across the top.

Every prompt is self-contained (the style block is repeated in each one), so you can paste any of them straight into Flow. Set the aspect ratio to **16:9 (landscape)**.

**Tips**
- For the same character in every thumbnail, add your best result as a reference image and start the next prompt with "Same character as the reference image."
- If Flow misspells the title text, delete the `TEXT:` line, add "no text, leave the top third empty dark space", and set the words yourself in Canva (font: *Bangers*, *Luckiest Guy* or *Permanent Marker*, white, black drop shadow).
- Generate 4 versions of each and pick the one where the face reads clearly at phone size.

---

## 1. Day 0

**Title:** You Keep Going Back to Day 0. · **Text:** `DAY 0.`

```
YouTube thumbnail, 16:9, 2D cartoon illustration, bold thick black outlines, cel shading with soft painterly shadows, cinematic and moody. Main character: a bald round-headed cartoon man with smooth cream-beige skin, no hair, no nose, huge expressive eyes with red veins and dark heavy bags underneath, thick angry-sad eyebrows, small sweat drops on his forehead.

Scene: late at night in a dark bedroom, he lies in bed under a rumpled blanket, gripping a glowing smartphone close to his face. The phone throws harsh red-orange light across his face, the rest of the room is near black. His expression: shock and guilt, eyes wide and teary, teeth clenched, as if he just realised he relapsed again. Behind him on the wall, a paper calendar with a long row of days crossed out in red marker, the last day torn off.

TEXT at the top in huge bold white hand-painted brush lettering with a subtle black shadow, all caps, centered, filling the top third: "DAY 0."

Style: high contrast, deep shadows, one strong light source, character fills the lower two thirds of the frame, close-up, emotional, clean and readable at small size.
```
